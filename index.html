About me

Where software meets business

I'm a Computer Engineering and Digital Technology (CEDT) student at Chulalongkorn University. I grew up around my family's business, so I've always cared about the problems behind the code: how orders come in, how prices are set and how reports get made.

That shaped how I work. I like starting from a real operational problem, understanding the business, then building the software or analysis that fixes it. I've done that as a Business Analyst at The Red Carbon, a Data Analyst at the Bank of Thailand, and in MTMS, the full-stack system I'm building for a multi-platform e-commerce business.
